The Star Festival

Tonight's the night of the Star Festival!

*Mario wakes up in the morning. He looks to the left to see Luigi’s bed empty. Mario gets up and walks into the kitchen to see Luigi.* Luigi: Morning, Mario! Mario: Morning… Luigi: Tonight’s the Star Festival! I’m going to go get the mail. Mario: Okay… *Luigi comes back inside with a large stack of envelopes* Mario: What’s with all the envelopes? Luigi: I don’t know. Most of this looks like spam. Mario: Hey, what’s this? *he pulls a red letter out from the middle of the stack, coming dangerously close to the stack falling over* Mario: It’s a letter from the Princess! “Dear Mario and Luigi, tonight’s the Star Festival. Please come to the castle to help us prepare. -Peach”. Well, let’s get going! *walks off* Luigi: …I’m kind of stuck here? *As they walk through Toad Town, Toads greet them. Blue and Yellow Toad come up and walk along with them.* Blue Toad: Hi, guys! Yellow Toad: The Star Festival’s tonight! I’m really excited! Blue Toad: It’s our first time seeing it for real! *They come up to the castle grounds entrance. Toads are running around with supplies and are putting up decorations. The Bros and Toads come up to the castle door.* Guard Toad: Welcome to the castle. Can we see your ID cards? *They all show the guards their ID cards and enter the castle. In the castle, there are also Toads working, and there’s a bunch of decorations up.* Peach: Hello! We’ve got lots of stuff to do for the Star Festival tonight! Where’s Yoshi? Mario: I thought I saw him back there… *the door opens to reveal Yoshi, who’s seemingly out of breath.* Yoshi: (I’m…here…) Peach: There you are. Luigi and Yoshi, you two need to get Yoshi Fruits. Blue and Yellow Toad, you two help the others in the kitchen— *a burst of flames comes from the kitchen doors. The smoke quickly dissipates* Peach: Yeah…you should probably keep an eye on them. And Mario, we’ll go over the guest list. Follow me! *Everyone goes to do their jobs.* Luigi: Hey, Yoshi? Where do we go for the fruits? *Yoshi signals for Luigi to get on his back, and then takes off. They come up to the garden with a ton of Yoshi Fruit bushes.* Luigi: Oh, wow. There’s a lot of them here! Let’s split up! *In the kitchen, it’s pure chaos. There’s dishes and silverware flying everywhere, Toads not really knowing how to cook trying to, and one Toad is using pots and pans and spoons as a drum kit.* Blue Toad: What are we even supposed to do about this? Yellow Toad: No idea… *Mario and Peach come up to the main Star Door, which opens up to reveal the throne room. The center table has a bunch of sticky notes on it.* Peach: So, this is everyone I could think of. Any suggestions? Mario: Hmm…I think Luigi would like to have E. Gadd here. Peach: Are you sure he wouldn’t bring some crazy invention again? Mario: What harm could he do? Peach: Remember the first trip to Isle Delfino? He let Bowser buy the paintbrush. Mario: Oh. Right. Peach: They didn’t even let us plead not guilty… Mario: Yeah. Anyways, this list looks good. But how do we get all the invites out? There are at least a hundred people on this list. Peach: Hmm…I’m not sure. Mario: The postal service! Wait, no. They’re closed today. But how’d they deliver your letter? Peach: Priority, mail. One of the perks of being a princess. We can assemble the whole crew to send invitations out. Mario: Well, let’s start writing the letters, then. *In the lobby, everything is peaceful. Mostly, there’s still a racket going on in the kitchen. Suddenly, the doors open and Daisy walks in* Daisy: I’m here! Oh. *suddenly, Blue and Yellow Toad run out of the kitchen door, followed by another flame* Blue: We need help… Yellow: It’s too much chaos. Daisy: Okay! Let’s do this! *in the kitchen, Daisy and the Toads walk in* Daisy: Okay! Everyone listen up! *all the chaos stops* Tonight’s the Star Festival, and we’ve got to cook a ton of food! So you start getting the ingredients mixed, and— Toad: We’re missing Yoshi Fruit. Daisy: Well, where are they? Blue Toad: Luigi and Yoshi are getting them. Daisy: *sigh* *At the garden, Luigi and Yoshi meet up* Luigi: I got a ton of fruits! I even found a wheelbarrow, to hold more! How many did you get? *Yoshi shows his basket to Luigi, and there’s only the stems and a few drops of the juice in the basket* Luigi: Did you eat them all again? Yoshi: *shrugs* Luigi: Let’s go back. Daisy: Looks like everything’s running smoothly. Just waiting on the fruits now. Blue Toad: They should be back any minute now. Luigi: Here’s the fruits! Daisy: There you are! Luigi: Oh–hi! I didn’t know you were here. Daisy: I wasn’t up until half an hour ago. Everything should be running smoothly now that these fruits are here. *Yoshi attempts to take one of the fruits, but Yellow Toad stops him.* Luigi: Yoshi ate all the fruits he was supposed to get, but I think this is enough. *It’s now evening. Guests are starting to come in, and a part of the big, grassy field has been sectioned off. Boshi comes in, and Yoshi greets him. They do some sort of complicated handshake* Peach: Alright. Everything’s pretty much finished. Now we just wait. Mario: Hopefully Bowser doesn’t come in and ruin this again. Peach: Again? Luigi: What are you talking about, bro? Mario: Nevermind. *Paper Mario walks in, and Mario comes and greets him.* Mario: Hey! *they try and handshake but Paper Mario’s hand just gets crumpled up.* Mario: Oh, sorry. I forgot. *Paper Mario shrugs and shakes his hand out, returning it to normal.* Blue Toad: Only 30 minutes left! *It’s finally starting to get dark, but then, an alarm sounds.* Alarm: BOWSER ALERT. TAKE COVER IMMEDIATELY. Mario: Oh, no. Bowser *from the front gate*: I just wanna deliver some flowers to my true love. *Mario glares at Bowser from the castle balcony. The guard Toads reject it and Bowser leaves angrily.* Peach: That was close. Yellow Toad: The starbits are starting to rain down! Blue Toad: I wonder if these taste like candy? Rosalina *magically appearing behind the group*: They do. Luigi: AHH—! Blue Toad: Oh, I want to try. *he catches one, but it disintegrates before he can try it* Aw… Mario: Let’s head down to the field. *The festival starts. Everyone’s watching all the starbits. You can see all the stars and even the galaxy clearly. It’s a magical, surreal experience.* Mario: This is truly a once-in-a-lifetime experience. And I get to spend it with my best friends and family! *At some point, Daisy gestures for Luigi to lay flat on the grass with her. Yoshi got more berries, and is sharing them with a Baby Yoshi. Even Bowser and Bowser Jr. are watching from outside the kingdom. DK is trying to catch the stardust. Blue Toad is recording the whole experience with a camera.* Luigi: Look! There’s Rainbow Road! *Slowly, everyone starts to leave as the festival draws to a close. It’s now early morning, still dark. The last of the starbits fall onto the planet.*

THE END.
