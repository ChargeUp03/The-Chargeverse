In the Castle Walls

Mario finds something hiding in the walls of Peach's castle...

*one rainy day, Mario and Peach are walking through the castle, until Mario notices a line in the wall* Mario: Hey, why's there a line in the wall? Peach: I'm not really supposed to show anyone this, so don't tell anyone, okay? *she puts her hand next to the crack, and the wall opens to reveal an entire living room, kitchen, and bedroom inside* Mario: What? You have an entire house in here? Peach: In case Bowser tries to do something. And then he ends up stealing the entire castle when I try to go in here. I'll show you around. Mario: Oka-OWOWOWOWOWOW! *Mario hit an invisible electric barrier in the doorway. He got instantly shrunken down to small form* Mario: * burnt* ...ow... Peach: Sorry, I forgot about that. Only I'm supposed to go in here, it zaps everybody else. Here's a mushroom. Mario: *takes the mushroom* That's better, thanks Peach: Anyway, here's the living room! Mario: How big is that TV? *in front of them is a massive 110-inch TV* Peach: Over 100 inches. Apparently, this is one of 5 in the entire world. Mario: Wow. I feel tiny next to this thing. Peach: This is the kitchen. Small compared to the main one, but it works. Mario: There’s nothing in this pantry but popcorn. Peach: Oh yeah, we’ve been meaning to stock that. Speaking of popcorn, why don’t we watch a movie? Mario: Let’s watch our new one! It should look amazing on the big screen! Peach: That’s a great idea! Let’s do it!

THE END.
