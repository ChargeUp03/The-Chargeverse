Flying to Mario Kart World

Mario and co. fly to Kart Island to partake in the Mario Kart World tournament!

*a bus pulls up to the airport. In it are Mario, Luigi, Blue and Yellow Toad, Toadette, Peach, Daisy, Yoshi, and a bunch of Toads. They all get out, besides the Toad driving, but he’s going to park the bus and meet back up. Everyone has 2 bags but Peach, who brought a concerning amount that Yellow Toad has to carry.* Mario: Here we are! The airport! Luigi: What if they lose our bags! Mario: It’ll be fine. Blue Toad: We do have a pretty big party, though. *Mario glares at Blue Toad* Blue Toad: What? *they all get through security, but one of the Toads are pulled aside for trying to take a Master Sword. They then get their tickets, and all the tickets are connected still; it’s going down onto the floor and around a corner* Mario: We’re all at gate…1? Intercom: Flight MA605 first class is now boarding. Luigi: That’s us! We’re at gate 10! Mario: RUN! *they all run to the gate, just barely making it* Attendant: Welcome aboard Mushroom Airlines Flight 605, going from the Mushroom Kingdom International Airport to Kart Island Airport. The flight will take 7 hours. Luigi: Seven hours? Mario: You knew it’d be a long flight to the island. Yellow Toad *from behind their seat* but it’ll be worth it! Peach: We get to race in Mario Kart World! Yoshi: (And it’s been 8 years since we’ve raced, right?) Luigi: *sigh*

THE END.
