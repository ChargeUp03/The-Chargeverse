Mushroom Kingdom School

As it turns out, school in the Mushroom Kingdom only goes up to 8th grade. Unfortunately, it now is not.

*As Mario and Peach walk through the town, a Toad comes up to them* News Toad: Princess! Master Toadsworth requests a meeting! All the Rulers are on their way now! Peach: …Okay… Toadsworth: Thank you for attending this meeting. I’m here to propose the idea of furthering the education system by an additional 4 grades. *Peach, Daisy, Pauline, and Florian all sigh* Mario: …Do I need to do this too? I have school in my world already. Toadsworth: All residents of the Mushroom Kingdom younger than 18 years of age would be required to attend education. Mario: Aw! Toadsworth: Are the Council members on board with this? *most of them agree* Rosalina: What’s wrong with this? I’d like to keep learning. Mario: School in my world is the most boring thing imaginable. Is it any different here? Peach: History, science, language arts… Mario: No math?! Peach: Basic math at the start, but no. Mario: YES! *They're all sitting in a classroom now, learning about the history of the Mushroom Kingdom. Daisy is already passed out on her desk, and everyone (but Rosalina) is bored* Luigi: Too much information! Mario: Beats learning about ancient computers. Peach: Learning about ancient computers would beat this, for me. Rosalina: Shh! Teacher: Do you four have something to share with the class? Mario: Nope. We’re talking about…*looks at board* how the Mushroom Kingdom got its name!

THE END.
