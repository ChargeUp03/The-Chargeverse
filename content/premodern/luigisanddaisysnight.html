Luigi and Daisy's Night

The start of a permanent relationship?

*a while after prom…* Peach: Wow. These new things are really cool! Mario: Yeah, phones are awesome! They’ve even got this cool bird game! But they’re a bit useless without towers. Peach: Well, I’m sure the Toads will figure it out. Luigi: *peeking into the classroom* Hey, Professor? Prof. Gadd: Luigi! Welcome back! Luigi: I’ve brought the phones! They need cell towers to actually call, though. Prof. Gadd: These are fascinating! I’ll figure out a solution to get them online immediately! Daisy: Hey, Luigi! Luigi: Oh, h-hi! Daisy! Daisy: You wanna come out here? I want to ask you something. Luigi: Uhh…y-yeah! J-just a minute. Sure is hot in here, huh? Prof. Gadd: It’s 102… Luigi: T-that explains it! Prof. Gadd: …Dots. Converted to Fahrenheit, that’s about 68 degrees. Daisy: Okay…I’ll just be outside. Prof. Gadd: Ah, school crushes. Am I right? Luigi: Wha—w-what are you talking about? *a few minutes later* Luigi: Hi. Daisy: Hey. Soooo, I was wondering if maybe you wanted to go out? Luigi: …L-like on a date? Daisy: Yeah. There’s a new restaurant that just opened up, I thought we could go there? Maybe? Or maybe not. I don’t know. Luigi: *to himself* (Yes! Say yes! Yes! Why am I not speaking? Speak!) Umm… Daisy: I’ll take that as a yes? Luigi: *nods* Daisy: Alright. Tonight, at 5. See ya there! *walks off* Mario: So you just said nothing? Luigi: I-I got nervous! My voice just leaves! I don’t know why! Mario: It’ll be fine! This is good, you’ll get to know each other better! Plus, you have a crush on her, right? Luigi: How do you know? Mario: …By observing. Not by reading your journal. Totally. Luigi: I guess I do… Mario: And she seems to have a crush on you! You’ve got this! Just find something nice to wear and go! Daisy: Does he not like me? Peach: I’m sure he does. Daisy: He just kinda stopped talking though! Peach: Mario said that Luigi gets nervous in situations. Daisy: That was a situation? Peach: Well—no, not like that. It’ll be okay, you’ll get to know each other better and… it’ll just work out! And shouldn’t you be the one encouraging me? Daisy: Yeah, probably, haha… *Luigi’s now standing outside, waiting. Mario’s watching from the bushes, unbeknownst to Luigi* Daisy: Sorry I’m late. Ready? Luigi: Uh—yeah! *also unbeknownst to Daisy, Peach is watching from another bush* Daisy: So, what was up with the glasses? Luigi: Oh, that? I went to the eye doctor a couple days ago and they said I needed them. Mario laughed the first time… Daisy: Just put sunglasses over them! It’ll make you look cooler. Luigi: Heh, I guess… *Mario walks into the restaurant* Mario: Hi. Just a table for one. Preferably by the couple that just walked in. Peach: *from the bush, to herself* Mario? Daisy: What, so was the computer just a rock? Luigi: Pretty much, yeah. Ancient computers are surprisingly interesting, though! Peach: *after walking into the restaurant, to the waiter* Just meeting someone! Mario: Princess? Peach: Hi, Mario! Mario: What are you doing here? Peach: Looking out for Daisy. Mario: That’s funny, I’m looking out for Luigi. Luigi: I still can’t get over the fact that they only drive karts here! What if it rains? Daisy: Rain? What’s that? Luigi: Wait, you don’t know what rain is? Daisy: Hahahaha! No, I-I do! Ha! You actually fell for that! Hahahahaha! Luigi: Heh heh, yeah. *Mario and Peach spot Luigi and Daisy getting up to leave* Waiter: Hi, welcome to Yoshi’s! Can I take your order? Mario: Oh—sorry, we have to go! *they both follow Luigi and Daisy out* Waiter: … *outside* Daisy: Well, this was fun! Thanks for going out with me! Luigi: Yeah, definitely! We should do this again sometime. Daisy: For sure! See ya! Luigi: Bye! Mario: *walking up* Hey, Luigi! How’d it go? Peach: *rounding the corner* Oh, Daisy! Fancy meeting you here! How’d it go? Luigi/Daisy: It went great!

THE END.
